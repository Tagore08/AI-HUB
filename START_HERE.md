# START HERE
Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

Welcome to AI-HUB! This is your personal context hub for AI interactions.

## Where Everything Is
- **AGENTS.md**: Table of contents and core context.
- **INSTRUCTIONS/**: Rules and permissions for AI behavior.
- **KNOWLEDGE/**: Global reference materials.
- **PROJECTS/**: Active and archived tasks.
- **PROMPTS/**: Templates for task handoffs and roles.
- **OUTPUTS/**: Deliverables generated by AI.

## Starting a Project
Copy the `PROJECTS/TEMPLATES/PROJECT_TEMPLATE/` folder into `PROJECTS/ACTIVE/` and rename it. Fill in the `README.md` and `GOALS.md`.

## Using with ANY Chat App
1. Paste the content of `INSTRUCTIONS/PORTABLE_PREAMBLE.md` into the custom instructions or the start of your chat.
2. For each task, fill out `PROMPTS/STANDARD_TASK_CONTEXT.md` and paste it as your first prompt.

## AI Handoffs (Free Tier Limits)
When you hit a usage limit, fill in the bottom three fields of `STANDARD_TASK_CONTEXT.md` (DONE SO FAR, NEXT STEP, HANDOFF NOTES) and paste the updated template into a new chat to continue without repeating work.

## Using with Coding Agents
Point coding agents to this folder as their workspace. They must follow `INSTRUCTIONS/MASTER_RULES.md` and `INSTRUCTIONS/PERMISSIONS.md`.

## Cloud and Mobile
For Google Drive or mobile access, keep read-only copies of KNOWLEDGE, INSTRUCTIONS, PROMPTS, and OUTPUTS there. Active work should stay here.

## Maintenance
See `MAINTENANCE.md` for weekly and monthly upkeep tasks.
